# What each role is offered

Generated by `apps/api/scripts/role_by_screen.py`; do not edit by hand. `apps/api/tests/test_the_role_by_screen_table_is_current.py` fails when this file and the code disagree.

**What it reads.** The role templates in `apps/api/core/permissions.py` (what `rbac()` falls back to when a person has no grant of their own), the workspace tiles and their role rules (`apps/web/lib/workspace/workspaceConfig.ts`, `apps/web/lib/auth/permissions.ts`), each firm panel's entries and the permission each asks, the Settings links' roles (`apps/web/lib/settings/groups.ts`) and the client workspace's sections (`apps/web/lib/workspace/ClientNavContext.tsx`).

**What it does not read.** A per-person grant or block (the Team screen's grid) changes what one person is offered and is not here. A page drawn for a role may still narrow its rows to the clients the person is assigned to (a Partner sees every client; every other role sees only assigned ones), hide a button the person may not use, or be refused by the server. A Client-portal contact and an employee are not staff and have their own portals, which read only their own client's or their own records (see `docs/design-record/tenancy-and-security.md`).

`yes` means the role is offered it; `-` that it is not.
