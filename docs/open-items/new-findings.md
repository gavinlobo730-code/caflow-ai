# New findings: everything found from 6 November 2026 on

**Why a separate list.** On 7 November 2026 the owner asked for what is found while working the backlog to be kept apart, so that the backlog's number (the list the owner knew on the evening of 5 November 2026: 487 open) shows progress on its own and the new findings are read as a second list. Nothing is hidden: every line here is a real finding, in the same format, with the same ids (never renumbered) and counted in the README beside the backlog.

**The rule.**
1. A finding made from 6 November 2026 on is written HERE and not in a section file. Ids continue the existing numbering (`POST-A-NNN`, `POST-B-NNN`, and so on: the prefix says when and who, as everywhere in this ledger).
2. Claude works the backlog's Claude-alone items first and comes to this list once they are done. Three things are not deferred, and each is said when it is taken: a defect in the change in flight (fixed in that change, not logged), a security gap, and a wrong figure or a false statement on a screen.
3. Closing a line works as it does for the backlog: delete the line in the same commit as the work, put the closure in `checked-closed.md`, run `python3 scripts/open_items_counts.py`, and add the day's row to `progress.md`.
4. `POST-A-381` to `POST-A-413` were moved here on 7 November 2026 from `post-demo-A-mine.md`, unchanged. Their ids and text are as they were.

Line format: as in the section files (`ID · theme · priority · effort [· UNSURE] [· live session] [· waits on owner] — **Title.** Detail. _When: reason._ — _Sources:_ … — _Refs:_ …`).
