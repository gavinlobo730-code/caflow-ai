### S50. Gratuity under the Code: the cap, the number of days and the wage base

- **Read on 19 November 2026 (the Code, section 53 and section 164(1); the Central Rules, rules 31 to 34, row D12):** the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 is one of the nine enactments the Code repeals (section 164(1), item 6), and the Payment of Gratuity (Central) Rules, 1972 were superseded by the Central Rules of 8 May 2026. Section 53 now governs. Gratuity is payable after five years of continuous service (three for a working journalist), and the five years are not needed on death, disablement or the end of a fixed-term contract (section 53(1)); it is "fifteen days' wages or such number of days as may be notified by the Central Government, based on the rate of wages last drawn" for every completed year or part over six months, a monthly rate divided by twenty-six and multiplied by the days (53(2) and Explanation 3), pro rata for a fixed-term employee (third proviso); and "the amount of gratuity payable to an employee shall not exceed such amount as may be notified by the Central Government" (53(3)). Forfeiture is section 53(6). The Rules add that a fixed-term employee is eligible after at least one year under the contract.
- **What the product does today (changed on 19-11-2026, night, in the gratuity pull request):** it computes gratuity by the formula that is the same in section 53 (fifteen days' wages, the monthly wage over twenty-six, a part of a year over six months counted as a year), with five years of service except on death or disablement and, for an employee recorded as on fixed-term employment whose term has expired, one year under the contract (rule 33(1)(a)); on basic pay plus dearness allowance (the repealed Act's wages), capped at Rs 20,00,000 (S.O. 1420(E) of 29-03-2018 under the repealed Act). A fixed-term employee who leaves before the term ends is not shown as eligible on one year, and the reason says why.
- **Question:** (1) Has a notification under section 53(3) fixed the cap (the old Rs 20 lakh or another figure), or under section 53(2) another number of days, or does the 2018 notification continue under section 164(2)(a)? (2) Does the Code's wage definition (section 2(88), with its one-half rule) now govern the gratuity base in place of basic pay plus dearness allowance? The Code says "wages" and the Rules do not carve gratuity out, but that is a legal reading. (3) Is a fixed-term employee who resigns or is dismissed before the term ends eligible on one year? Section 53(1)(b) makes a resignation payable after five years; the second proviso waives the five years where the termination "is due to ... expiration of fixed term employment"; rule 33(1)(a)'s second proviso says an employee on fixed term employment "shall be eligible for gratuity, if he renders service under the contract for a period of at least one year" and does not name expiry. The text read does not say how the two fit. (4) The tax exemption for gratuity under the Income-tax Act, 2025 is a separate question (D8, D9).
- **Needed:** any notification under section 53 of the Code from the Labour Ministry's lists (8 May 2026: the four entries headed "Publication of notification of the Code on Social Security 2020 ... regarding" with the title cut off, CG-DL-E-09052026-272371, 272376, 272377, 272378; 29 May 2026), or a Ministry statement; and a practising CA's reading on the wage base and on how the Rule's one-year sentence and the Code's second proviso fit for a fixed-term employee who leaves early.
