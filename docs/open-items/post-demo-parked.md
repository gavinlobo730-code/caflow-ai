# After the demo — Parked (waiting until someone asks, or until the work is sized down)

Parked items are real, are **not** counted as open in `README.md`, and keep their ids. An item is parked when the owner's rule of 9 October 2026 says so (`plan-going-faster-2026-10-09.md`: low priority and sized weeks or months, or a title that starts "Watch for", "Hold" or "Keep") **or** when Claude decided on 14 October 2026 to wait until a client or pilot firm asks (`decisions-taken-2026-10-14.md`, section 3). **A pre-demo item is never parked.**

IDs `POST-X-NNN` are stable: never renumber, and an item keeps the letter it was given (A Claude alone, B the owner decides, C only the owner or outsiders). To un-park an item, move its line back to the section file for its letter in the same commit and run `python3 scripts/open_items_counts.py`. To close one, delete its line here. The format is the same as the section files, which `apps/api/tests/test_the_open_items_ledger_is_well_formed.py` enforces.
